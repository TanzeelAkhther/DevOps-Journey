## Challenge lifecycle

- **Open:** not attempted.
- **In progress:** currently being worked.
- **Needs review:** submitted; review and feedback pending.
- **Passed:** acceptance criteria demonstrated.
- **Retry:** gaps remain; another attempt is required.

## Challenge design standard

Each challenge should include:
- A realistic scenario and clear objective.
- Constraints and acceptance criteria.
- Expected artifacts or observable behavior.
- Failure cases to test.
- Security and reliability considerations.
- A requirement to explain the solution and trade-offs.

Avoid turning every challenge into a step-by-step tutorial. Hints should be incremental, and the learner should attempt diagnosis before receiving the solution.
